\subsection{Results: Scaling Study}
\label{sec:results_scaling}

Quantitative results for all 54 configurations are shown in
Figure~\ref{fig:results_overview}, which plots all four metrics jointly across the
full experiment.
Each point corresponds to one configuration; colour encodes data fraction and marker
shape encodes generator size.
Four high-level patterns emerge: (i)~CPA MAE spans over an order of magnitude
($0.008$--$0.171$) and is far more discriminative than the perceptual metrics, which
saturate within a narrow band for well-behaved GAN configurations; (ii)~CycleDiffusion
attains systematically lower Patch-SSIM ($0.200$--$0.293$) than GAN-based methods
($0.298$--$0.349$), irrespective of scale; (iii)~two model families exhibit training
instability under specific scaling conditions; and (iv)~perceptual metric rankings
correlate only moderately with the CPA MAE ranking.

% -------------------------------------------------------
\label{sec:results_size}

We first examine how all four metrics respond to changes in generator capacity,
reporting each model family's nine configurations across the three data fractions.

\paragraph{CycleGAN.}
Patch-SSIM ($0.312$--$0.342$) and LPIPS ($0.038$--$0.040$) are near-invariant across
all nine CycleGAN configurations.
FID ranges from $193.3$ to $245.4$ with no monotonic size trend.
CPA MAE is the most size-sensitive metric: at the 100\% data fraction, the small,
medium, and large generators yield $0.049 \pm 0.042$, $0.008 \pm 0.007$, and
$0.085 \pm 0.032$ respectively.
At reduced data fractions, no consistent size-dependent trend in CPA MAE is observed.

\paragraph{UNIT.}
Patch-SSIM ($0.298$--$0.338$), LPIPS ($0.037$--$0.040$), and FID ($195$--$211$) show
no systematic variation with generator size.
At the 100\% data fraction, CPA MAE is $0.046 \pm 0.039$, $0.037 \pm 0.033$, and
$0.043 \pm 0.047$ for small, medium, and large generators.
Across all data fractions, CPA MAE ranges from $0.017$ to $0.050$ with no consistent
advantage for any single size.

\paragraph{MUNIT.}
All four metrics remain within narrow ranges across the full $3{\times}3$ grid:
Patch-SSIM $0.305$--$0.331$, LPIPS $0.039$--$0.040$, FID $193$--$210$, and CPA MAE
$0.040$--$0.059$.
At the 100\% data fraction, CPA MAE is $0.051 \pm 0.023$, $0.043 \pm 0.014$, and
$0.055 \pm 0.051$ for small, medium, and large generators respectively.

\paragraph{DCLGAN.}
At the 100\% data fraction, all three sizes produce stable output: Patch-SSIM
$0.322$--$0.349$, LPIPS $0.038$--$0.039$, FID $190$--$212$, and CPA MAE
$0.028$--$0.042$.
The large generator achieves the lowest FID of any configuration in the study
($190.0$, CPA MAE $0.039 \pm 0.034$).
At the 50\% and 25\% data fractions, the large generator produces collapsed output:
Patch-SSIM $0.126$--$0.143$, LPIPS $0.046$--$0.049$, FID $327$--$363$, and CPA MAE
$0.082$--$0.091$.
Small and medium generators remain stable at all three data fractions.

\paragraph{UVCGAN.}
The small generator is stable across all data fractions: Patch-SSIM $0.311$--$0.337$,
LPIPS $0.038$--$0.039$, FID $208$--$253$, and CPA MAE $0.035$--$0.077$.
Medium and large generators show substantially degraded performance: Patch-SSIM falls
to $0.142$--$0.222$, FID rises to $286$--$395$, and CPA MAE reaches $0.063$--$0.118$.
At the 100\% data fraction specifically, the medium and large generators yield CPA MAE
of $0.118 \pm 0.053$ and $0.063 \pm 0.037$ alongside FID of $343.2$ and $286.5$.

\paragraph{CycleDiffusion.}
CycleDiffusion produces consistently lower Patch-SSIM ($0.200$--$0.293$) and higher
LPIPS ($0.041$--$0.048$) than all GAN-based families across all nine configurations.
At the 100\% data fraction, CPA MAE is $0.051 \pm 0.029$, $0.082 \pm 0.071$, and
$0.055 \pm 0.043$ for small, medium, and large generators.
The large generator at 100\% data also shows elevated FID ($322.1$).

\paragraph{Cross-model observation.}
For the four families without training instability (CycleGAN, UNIT, MUNIT,
CycleDiffusion), Patch-SSIM ($0.298$--$0.349$) and LPIPS ($0.037$--$0.048$) remain
within a narrow band across generator sizes, while CPA MAE spans a considerably
wider range ($0.008$--$0.087$) across the same configurations.


% -------------------------------------------------------
\label{sec:results_data}

We next examine the effect of training data fraction on all four metrics, with
particular attention to the 100\% fraction from which one model size per family is
selected for ensemble uncertainty estimation in Section~\ref{sec:uncertainty}.

\paragraph{CycleGAN.}
Patch-SSIM, LPIPS, and FID show no consistent improvement with additional training
data across any generator size.
At the 100\% data fraction, the medium generator yields CPA MAE $0.008 \pm 0.007$,
compared with $0.057 \pm 0.033$ at 50\% and $0.053 \pm 0.051$ at 25\%.
For the small and large generators, CPA MAE shows no consistent trend with data
fraction, ranging $0.045$--$0.063$ and $0.052$--$0.085$ respectively.

\paragraph{UNIT.}
Patch-SSIM, LPIPS, and FID are stable across all data fractions for all three sizes.
At the 100\% data fraction, CPA MAE is $0.046 \pm 0.039$, $0.037 \pm 0.033$, and
$0.043 \pm 0.047$ for small, medium, and large generators.
The small generator achieves a lower CPA MAE at 50\% data ($0.017 \pm 0.020$) than at
100\%, and the large generator yields $0.023$--$0.024$ at 50\% and 25\% data.

\paragraph{MUNIT.}
Patch-SSIM, LPIPS, and FID remain stable across data fractions for all three sizes.
At the 100\% data fraction, CPA MAE is $0.051 \pm 0.023$, $0.043 \pm 0.014$, and
$0.055 \pm 0.051$ for small, medium, and large generators.
The medium generator shows a monotonic decrease in CPA MAE from 25\% to 100\% data
($0.059 \to 0.053 \to 0.043$); the small and large generators do not follow this
pattern.

\paragraph{DCLGAN.}
At the 100\% data fraction, all three generators are stable: Patch-SSIM $0.322$--$0.349$,
LPIPS $0.038$--$0.039$, FID $190$--$212$, and CPA MAE $0.028$--$0.042$.
At the 50\% data fraction, the large generator collapses (Patch-SSIM $0.126$, FID
$326.9$, CPA MAE $0.082 \pm 0.061$), and at 25\% the collapse persists (Patch-SSIM
$0.143$, FID $362.8$, CPA MAE $0.091 \pm 0.078$).
Small and medium generators remain stable across all three data fractions, with CPA
MAE ranging $0.019$--$0.049$.

\paragraph{UVCGAN.}
At the 100\% data fraction, the small generator yields Patch-SSIM $0.311$, LPIPS
$0.039$, FID $242.5$, and CPA MAE $0.035 \pm 0.021$.
The medium generator is degraded at all data fractions, with its highest CPA MAE
occurring at 100\% data ($0.118 \pm 0.053$, FID $343.2$).
The large generator also remains degraded across data fractions (CPA MAE
$0.063$--$0.095$, FID $286$--$395$).

\paragraph{CycleDiffusion.}
At the 100\% data fraction, CPA MAE is $0.051 \pm 0.029$, $0.082 \pm 0.071$, and
$0.055 \pm 0.043$ for small, medium, and large generators, with Patch-SSIM
$0.217$--$0.270$ and FID $210$--$322$.
The small generator's CPA MAE rises from $0.051$ at 100\% data to $0.171 \pm 0.045$
at 25\%.
The medium generator achieves its lowest CPA MAE at 25\% data ($0.035 \pm 0.017$)
rather than at 100\% ($0.082 \pm 0.071$).

\paragraph{Cross-model observation.}
At the 100\% data fraction, CPA MAE for stable configurations ranges from $0.008$
(CycleGAN\,M) to $0.085$ (CycleGAN\,L), while Patch-SSIM and LPIPS remain within a
narrow band.
Moving from 50\% to 100\% data does not produce a consistent CPA MAE improvement
across model families or generator sizes.


% -------------------------------------------------------
\label{sec:results_joint}

\paragraph{Joint scaling behaviour.}
Figure~\ref{fig:results_overview} shows per-model results across the full
size~$\times$~data-fraction grid.
CycleGAN and UNIT each display a single low-MAE cell (medium/100\% and small/50\%
respectively), rather than a monotonically improving surface across the grid.
MUNIT shows the flattest landscape, with all metrics varying within a narrow range
across all nine cells.
DCLGAN's grid contains a sharp discontinuity at the large/50\% and large/25\% cells,
where FID and CPA MAE values are far outside the range of the remaining seven cells.
UVCGAN presents a two-region structure: the three small-size cells have substantially
lower FID and CPA MAE than the six medium and large-size cells.
For CycleDiffusion, the small/25\% and small/50\% cells are outliers with CPA MAE
exceeding $0.15$.

\paragraph{Metric agreement.}
Figure~\ref{fig:ranking_correlation} shows Spearman rank correlations between the four
metric orderings over all 54 configurations (rank~1 assigned to the best-performing
configuration per metric; all pairs significant at $p{<}0.001$).
Patch-SSIM and LPIPS rankings agree most strongly with each other ($\rho{=}0.82$).
FID shows weaker agreement with both Patch-SSIM ($\rho{=}0.60$) and LPIPS
($\rho{=}0.49$).
CPA MAE shows only moderate agreement with all three perceptual metrics:
$\rho{=}0.63$ with Patch-SSIM, $\rho{=}0.64$ with LPIPS, and $\rho{=}0.55$ with FID.
