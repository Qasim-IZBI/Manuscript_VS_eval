\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

The scaling study across 54 configurations and the subsequent ensemble analysis reveal
that the optimal scaling point is model-family-specific and non-trivially predictable
from perceptual metrics alone, and that inter-seed agreement across deep ensembles
separates the six families along an axis distinct from CPA~MAE.

\textbf{Perceptual saturation and the case for task-specific evaluation:}
Perceptual metrics saturate within a narrow band across well-behaved GAN configurations
(Patch-SSIM $0.30$--$0.35$, LPIPS $0.037$--$0.040$, FID $190$--$215$), with Spearman
rank correlations against CPA~MAE of only $r_s{=}0.63$, $0.64$, and $0.55$.
As a concrete illustration, CycleGAN's medium and large generators at 100\% data are
nearly indistinguishable by Patch-SSIM ($0.334$ vs.\ $0.342$), yet their CPA~MAE
differs by more than an order of magnitude ($0.008$ vs.\ $0.085$).
A perceptual-only selection workflow would treat the two as equivalent and propagate
the 10-fold CPA~MAE gap into any downstream fibrosis staging readout.
Consistent with prior calibration analyses of unpaired
translation~\cite{trustworthy_i2i2025}, these findings confirm that task-specific
evaluation is non-negotiable for virtual staining model selection.

\textbf{Minimum viable configurations across scaling:}
CPA~MAE does not improve smoothly with scale: a few configurations record substantially
lower error than their neighbours, partly because the metric carries high variance over
the $N{=}5$ test WSIs.
When the full training set is available, medium-capacity generators are the most reliable
choice across families.
MUNIT is the most stable family overall (CPA~MAE $0.040$--$0.059$ across all nine
conditions), consistent with AdaIN-based style disentanglement acting as an implicit
regulariser.
DCLGAN\,S remains stable across all data fractions (CPA~MAE $0.019$--$0.049$), making
it a sound choice under limited data.
CycleGAN and UNIT show non-monotonic data-fraction dependence and are less reliable when
training data is scarce.

\textbf{Training instability:}
DCLGAN and UVCGAN each exhibit catastrophic failure modes that are invisible until they
occur.
DCLGAN's large generator collapses at reduced data fractions (FID $327$--$363$,
Patch-SSIM $0.13$--$0.14$) while smaller variants remain stable, reflecting insufficient
patch density for the dual InfoNCE contrastive buffer.
UVCGAN's two-stage schedule~\cite{torbunov2022uvcgan} does not scale beyond the small
generator in this data regime; medium and large variants fail across most data fractions
(FID up to $395$, Patch-SSIM as low as $0.14$).
Both failures manifest as sharp discontinuities rather than gradual degradation, so
monitoring FID throughout training is essential for early detection.

\textbf{CycleDiffusion as a distinct generative paradigm:}
CycleDiffusion's consistently lower Patch-SSIM ($0.200$--$0.293$ vs.\ $0.298$--$0.349$
for stable GANs) and higher LPIPS reflect the DDIM inversion inductive bias rather
than failure: the noise-space bottleneck enforces domain-invariant structure at the cost
of per-tile pixel sharpness.
The small generator's CPA~MAE rises sharply with data reduction ($0.051$ at 100\%
$\to$ $0.171$ at 25\%), consistent with DDIM inversion requiring well-covered training
distributions to produce stable noise representations.
Counter-intuitively, the medium generator achieves its lowest CPA~MAE at 25\% data
($0.035$ vs.\ $0.082$ at 100\%), suggesting limited diversity acts as a regulariser
against slide-specific colour artefacts.
At 100\% data, the small generator (CPA~MAE $0.051$) is selected for ensemble
uncertainty estimation; the medium generator is preferable under data reduction.

\textbf{Inter-seed agreement across families:}
The per-tile mean ensemble variance (Sec.~\ref{sec:unc_variance_results}) separates
the six families along an axis that CPA~MAE alone does not surface.
Among GAN families, DCLGAN's IQR ($0.043$) is roughly half that of any other GAN
variant, consistent with the dual InfoNCE objective acting as an architectural
regulariser that anchors translations to shared content features across seeds.
Paired with its leading across-tile calibration (Pearson $\rho = 0.61$, ECE $0.081$;
Supplementary Sec.~\ref{sec:supp-unc-results}), DCLGAN combines low task error with the most informative
ensemble-variance signal among the six families.
CycleGAN's selected medium configuration pairs the study-low CPA~MAE with moderate
inter-seed spread, making it the primary candidate when raw task accuracy outweighs
fine-grained uncertainty information.
MUNIT's highest median uncertainty among GAN families ($3.69$) is the inter-seed
counterpart of the AdaIN-based stability noted above: style disentanglement preserves
task accuracy while admitting inter-seed style variation.
CycleDiffusion's profile is qualitatively different: it records the lowest median
uncertainty ($3.16$) yet the widest IQR ($0.238$).
The shared-noise-space conditioning of DDIM inversion drives ensemble members toward
a near-deterministic output where the conditioning is unambiguous, suppressing
inter-seed variation more strongly than any GAN.
On structurally ambiguous tiles the same reverse process diverges sharply, and the
resulting uncertainty signal is uncorrelated with cycle error ($\rho = {-}0.03$).
The diffusion family is therefore best read as conditionally consistent rather than
uniformly more reliable than the GAN alternatives.

\textbf{Limitations:}
The dataset covers a single tissue type, disease model, and staining laboratory;
multi-centre validation is required to establish generalisability.
The held-out test set of $N{=}5$ WSIs provides limited statistical power: configurations
with CPA~MAE $0.008$ carry a standard deviation of $0.007$, so fine-grained rankings
among the best configurations should be interpreted with caution.
Patch-SSIM and LPIPS are computed on loosely registered adjacent-section pairs,
inflating apparent perceptual error for structurally correct but misaligned translations.
The uncertainty framework captures epistemic uncertainty only; aleatoric uncertainty
from staining variability and biological heterogeneity between sections is not modelled.
