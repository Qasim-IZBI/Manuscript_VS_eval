\section{Dataset}
\label{sec:dataset}

The dataset used in this study comprises whole-slide images of mouse liver tissue
from a bile-duct ligation~(BDL) model of cholestatic liver disease~\cite{ghallab2025asbt},
spanning four post-surgical time points that recapitulate a progression from minimal
to advanced hepatic fibrosis.
For each of 36 C57BL/6N mice, serial sections from the same tissue block were
stained with H\&E and Sirius Red~(SR), the diagnostic gold standard for hepatic
collagen quantification~\cite{huang2013sirius}.
Because the two sections are non-adjacent cuts, the dataset is inherently \emph{unpaired}.
After whole-slide digitisation at $\times$20 magnification, WSIs were tiled into
non-overlapping $512{\times}512$\,pixel patches and downsampled to $256{\times}256$\,pixels,
yielding an effective resolution of $0.442\,\mu$m/pixel; the final collection comprises approximately
192{,}847 H\&E and 183{,}253 SR training tiles across 30 WSIs, with 16{,}285
tiles per domain reserved for testing, partitioned at the animal level to
prevent data leakage.
To the best of our knowledge, this constitutes the first publicly available resource
for unsupervised H\&E~$\to$~SR translation in liver tissue; full acquisition and
preprocessing details, including animal model, histological staining protocol,
WSI acquisition, tiling, and data splits, are provided in Supplementary Sec.~1.
