\section{Models}
\label{sec:models}

We evaluate six unsupervised image-to-image translation architectures, summarised in
Table~\ref{tab:model_overview}.
All six share a cycle-consistency training objective; five are GAN-based and use a
$70{\times}70$ PatchGAN discriminator.
\textbf{CycleGAN}~\cite{zhu2017unpaired} serves as the plain adversarial baseline.
Building on this, \textbf{UNIT}~\cite{liu2017unsupervised} introduces a shared VAE
latent space, while \textbf{MUNIT}~\cite{huang2018multimodal} disentangles content from
style via Adaptive Instance Normalisation to support multimodal generation.
\textbf{DCLGAN}~\cite{han2021dual} augments cycle-consistency with a dual contrastive
patch objective, and \textbf{UVCGAN}~\cite{torbunov2022uvcgan} replaces the ResNet
generator with a UNet--ViT~\cite{dosovitskiy2021vit} hybrid backbone and adds masked-image pretraining.
The sixth model, \textbf{CycleDiffusion}~\cite{wu2022cyclediffusion}, departs from the
GAN paradigm entirely: it enforces cycle consistency through a shared diffusion noise
space, encoding source images deterministically via DDIM inversion~\cite{song2021ddim}
and decoding them into the target domain via DDIM sampling.
Each architecture is trained at three capacity scales (${\sim}10$\,M, ${\sim}50$\,M, and
${\sim}100$\,M parameters), yielding 54 configurations in total; full architectural
details and size configurations are provided in Supplementary Sec.~2.

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\caption{Overview of the six evaluated models. \textit{Inference} distinguishes
  single forward-pass generators from iterative reverse-diffusion samplers.
  Full architectural details and loss formulations are provided in Supplementary Sec.~2.}
\label{tab:model_overview}
\begin{tabular}{llll}
\toprule
\textbf{Model} & \textbf{Family} & \textbf{Generator backbone} & \textbf{Inference} \\
\midrule
CycleGAN      & Cycle-consistent GAN              & ResNet           & Single-pass \\[2pt]
UNIT          & Shared-latent-space GAN           & VAE--ResNet      & Single-pass \\[2pt]
MUNIT         & Content--style disentanglement GAN & AdaIN--ResNet   & Single-pass \\[2pt]
DCLGAN        & Contrastive learning GAN          & ResNet           & Single-pass \\[2pt]
UVCGAN        & Hybrid ViT GAN                    & UNet--ViT        & Single-pass \\[2pt]
CycleDiffusion & Shared-latent diffusion          & Dual UNet (DDPM) & Iterative ($2{\times}$DDIM) \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}
