\section{Models}
\label{sec:models}

\begin{figure*}[t!]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=0.82\linewidth]{images/I2I_Models_02}
  \caption{%
    Schematic encoder--decoder architectures of the six evaluated unsupervised I2I models.
    \textit{CycleGAN} and \textit{DCLGAN} share a plain ResNet generator backbone;
    DCLGAN additionally enforces a dual patch-level contrastive loss between domains.
    \textit{UNIT} couples the two directions through a shared VAE bottleneck.
    \textit{MUNIT} adds a per-domain style encoder that injects style codes via
    Adaptive Instance Normalisation, enabling multimodal generation.
    \textit{UVCGAN} replaces the ResNet bottleneck with a Vision Transformer block
    preceded by masked-image pretraining.
    \textit{CycleDiffusion} departs from the GAN paradigm: each domain has a
    dedicated DDPM whose shared noise space mediates the cycle, with translation
    performed via DDIM inversion and sampling rather than a deterministic forward pass.%
  }
  \label{fig:model_architectures}
\end{figure*}

We evaluate six unsupervised I2I architectures (Figure~\ref{fig:model_architectures}):
\textbf{CycleGAN}~\cite{zhu2017unpaired}, \textbf{UNIT}~\cite{liu2017unsupervised},
\textbf{MUNIT}~\cite{huang2018multimodal}, \textbf{DCLGAN}~\cite{han2021dual}, and
\textbf{UVCGAN}~\cite{torbunov2022uvcgan} are GAN-based families that share a
cycle-consistency objective and differ in latent-space structure, contrastive objectives,
and generator backbone (see Supplementary Sec.~2 for loss formulations);
\textbf{CycleDiffusion}~\cite{wu2022cyclediffusion} instead enforces cycle consistency
through a shared diffusion noise space via DDIM inversion~\cite{song2021ddim} and
sampling.
Each is trained at three capacity scales (${\sim}10$\,M, ${\sim}50$\,M, and ${\sim}100$\,M
parameters), yielding 54 configurations in total; full architectural details are given in
Supplementary Sec.~2.
